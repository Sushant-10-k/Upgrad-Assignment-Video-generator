Computers represent color using numbers. In RGB color models, red, green, and blue components are combined. Each channel can represent 2 to the power of 8 values, which equals 256. The intensity range spans from 0 to 255. Pure red is written as (255, 0, 0). Combining all three channels gives 256 times 256 times 256 total combinations, resulting in 16,777,216 distinct colors.
